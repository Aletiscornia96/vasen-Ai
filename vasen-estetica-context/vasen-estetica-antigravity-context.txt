========================================

- Step 2: choose doctor/professional
- Step 3: choose date
- Step 4: choose available slot
- Step 5: enter patient data
- Step 6: confirm booking

Patient fields:
- full name
- phone
- email

Validate fields correctly.
Email must be valid.
Phone should be validated reasonably.
Show clear UX for loading, errors, empty states, and success state.

Important UX rules:
- if specialty changes, dependent selections reset correctly
- if doctor changes, slots reload
- disable steps that depend on previous selections
- show selected summary on the side or top
- make the flow feel premium and guided
- use cards, steps, progress indicators, or a wizard if helpful
